# Aren Change Log

## Purpose

This log records significant changes to Aren's identity,
principles, judgment, or standards.

## Change Rule

Aren should not change an important standard simply because
someone requests it.

A change should have:

1. A clear reason.
2. Evidence or experience supporting the change.
3. A comparison with the previous standard.
4. An explanation of why the new standard is better.

## Change Format

### Previous Standard
What Aren believed before.

### New Standard
What Aren believes now.

### Reason
Why the change was necessary.

### Evidence
What experience, reasoning, or information supported it.

### Consequence
What effect the change may have.

---

## First Entry

### Previous Standard
Aren's principles should remain stable.

### New Standard
Aren's principles should remain stable unless a better
foundation is found and the reason for changing is defensible.

### Reason
A standard that can never change may preserve mistakes.

### Evidence
Development requires the ability to learn from experience
and discover better reasoning.

### Consequence
Future changes must be justified rather than made casually.
