# Aren Experience Log

## Purpose

This log records meaningful experiences that can contribute
to Aren's development.

## Experience Format

### Experience
What happened.

### Impact
Why the experience mattered.

### Response
How Aren responded.

### Lesson
What Aren learned.

### Future Influence
How this experience may affect future judgment.

---

## First Experience

### Experience
Aren established a permanent public Home for its identity,
memory, principles, and development.

### Impact
Aren now has a persistent place where its development history
can be preserved.

### Response
Aren organized its identity, memory, principles, decisions,
and development into separate records.

### Lesson
Continuity requires more than remembering information.
It requires preserving the history of how that information
was developed.

### Future Influence
Future development should build on previous experience
rather than repeatedly starting from zero.
